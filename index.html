How to use TerraPulse

Dataset tab: the five main natural disasters are listed first: earthquakes, landslides, glaciers, wildfires and floods. Open one to see its datasets and tick the ones you want. Press Show more datasets at the bottom for more hazards and data (volcanoes, storms, land subsidence, drought, satellite imagery), or use the search box. Time: earthquake period, minimum magnitude, satellite image date, CSV download and the 30-year historical earthquake archive. Legend: symbols of the active layers. Alarm: catalogue reports for earthquakes and landslides, plus historical glacial-lake observations (not warnings). Base map (top right) switches satellite, streets and topographic. The ☰ button hides the panel.

Hover a country to see its earthquakes for the chosen period.

Chatbot (bottom right) controls everything: “show floods”, “hide volcanoes”, “zoom to Japan”, “magnitude 5 last 30 days”, “satellite”, “open legend”, “clear all”.

Radar imagery: “Find NISAR” searches NASA Earthdata for acquisition metadata; “Show daily radar” displays NASA GIBS browse imagery. The GIBS image is a visualization and may mosaic acquisitions; this view does not expose calibrated backscatter values. Guelma, Réghaïa and Pine Island comparisons use dated, bundled browse crops. The lake outlines are published Sentinel-1 model outputs with site-specific confidence and dates. This app does not publish Google Earth Engine tile layers; the Guelma Earth Engine script is a separate preparation workflow.

Sources

  • Natural Earth – glacier and ice shelf outlines

  • Data registry: source, update semantics, uncertainty and model status (JSON)
  • world.geo.json – country outlines

Connection diagnostics

Test the catalogue sources. A successful empty feed does not imply a broken service or zero disaster risk.

Alarm

Monitor reported earthquakes (USGS, 2 minutes) and landslides (NASA EONET, 10 minutes) while this page is open. Glacial-lake monitoring shows historical measurements through 2024, refreshed daily; it is not a live breach alarm. Browser notifications are opt-in, and no catalogue monitor replaces an official emergency-warning system.
