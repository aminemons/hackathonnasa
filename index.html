How to use TerraPulse

Dataset tab: the five main natural disasters are listed first: earthquakes, landslides, glaciers, wildfires and floods. Open one to see its datasets and tick the ones you want. Press Show more datasets at the bottom for more hazards and data (volcanoes, storms, land subsidence, drought, satellite imagery), or use the search box. Time: earthquake period, minimum magnitude, satellite image date, CSV download and the 30-year historical earthquake archive. Legend: symbols of the active layers. Alarm: latest alerts for the five main hazards. Base map (top right) switches satellite, streets and topographic. The ☰ button hides the panel.

Hover a country to see its earthquakes for the chosen period.

Chatbot (bottom right) controls everything: “show floods”, “hide volcanoes”, “zoom to Japan”, “magnitude 5 last 30 days”, “satellite”, “open legend”, “clear all”.

SAR layers (floods, land subsidence, glacial motion) come from Sentinel-1 via Google Earth Engine. Their links expire, so regenerate layers.json when they go blank.
